Abstract

PromptForm AI is a full-stack SaaS platform that turns a one-sentence prompt or an uploaded document into a validated, shareable online form in seconds. A hybrid engine pairs Google Gemini with rule-based AIFormBrain checks, and the platform supports editing, quizzes, analytics and teamwork. All 50 automated tests passed.

Keywords: Artificial Intelligence · Form Generation · Next.js · Google Gemini · Full-Stack Development

Figure 1. Live landing page (Vercel deployment).

Introduction

  • Background: admissions, intake, feedback and quizzes run on web forms, yet every field is still added by hand.
  • Problem: a 10-field form took 15–25 min; field-type errors were common and PDF forms were re-typed.
  • Solution: generate a typed, validated form from a prompt or document and manage responses in one place.

Objectives

  • Generate: a complete form from a prompt in < 10 s.
  • Digitise: fields from PDF, DOCX, XLSX and images.
  • Build: ≥ 10 field types, visual builder, share link.
  • Assess: graded quizzes with anti-cheat and analytics.
  • Secure: JWT, team roles, ≥ 40 tests, cloud deployment.

Discussion

  • Faster: creation time fell from ≈ 15–25 min to ≈ 8–12 s.
  • Reliable: rule-based review fixed wrong field types and missing options; model fallback kept generation working during Gemini outages.
  • Secure: API tests exposed client-only checks, which were fixed server-side and re-verified.
  • Responsive: CRUD APIs took ≈ 40–200 ms and submissions ≈ 100–250 ms (target < 500 ms).

Conclusion

  • Summary: a deployed, secure platform that turns a prompt or document into a shareable form in seconds; all 10 objectives were met.
  • Applications: registrations, patient intake, surveys and classroom quizzes.
  • Limitations: Gemini dependence, 30–50 s cold starts on free hosting, no real payments.

Recommendations

  • Payments: Razorpay / Stripe billing.
  • Smarter AI: generated conditional logic.
  • Multilingual: voice-to-form, translation.
  • Integrations: Sheets, Slack, Zapier.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA audit.
