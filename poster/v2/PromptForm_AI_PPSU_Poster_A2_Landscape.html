01Abstract

PromptForm AI turns a one-sentence prompt or an uploaded document into a complete, validated and shareable online form in seconds. Google Gemini, guided by rule-based AIFormBrain checks, builds the form; users then edit, publish, run quizzes and analyse responses on one platform.

02Problem Statement

  • Manual: every field and rule set by hand
  • Slow: 15–25 min for a 10-field form
  • Error-prone: wrong types, re-typed PDFs
  • Fragmented: separate quiz & analytics tools

03Objectives

  • Prompt → complete form in under 10 s
  • Digitise PDF, DOCX, XLSX and image forms
  • 10+ field types with automatic validation
  • Graded quizzes, analytics and export
  • Secure, team-ready cloud deployment
