Introduction

Online forms run admissions, patient intake, surveys and quizzes, yet every field, type and rule is still built by hand.

Problem

A 10-field form takes 15–25 min; wrong field types are common and paper or PDF forms must be re-typed.

Objectives

  • Prompt → complete form in < 10 s
  • Digitise PDF, DOCX, XLSX & images
  • 10+ validated field types
  • Quizzes, analytics & export
  • Secure, team-ready cloud app

Conclusion

  • Impact: a deployed platform that builds validated forms in seconds.
  • Applications: registrations, patient intake, surveys and quizzes.
  • Limitations: relies on the Gemini API; 30–50 s cold start.
